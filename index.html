Good To Know

Quick answers before you arrive.

Do I need a costume?

No. Costumes are welcome if you want to dress up, but the event is open either way.

What should I bring?

Bring yourself, a friend if you want, and enough curiosity to try a few games.

Is it scary?

The theme is Halloween, but the tone is playful and youth-friendly.
